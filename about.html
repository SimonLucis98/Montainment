关于我们 / About Us

欢迎来到蒙堂木下

Montainment（蒙堂木下）是一个致力于将“学习”与“娱乐”完美结合的独立静态网站。我们相信，学习和放松并不冲突。在这里，你可以挑战语言测验，体验原生记忆小游戏，阅读连载小说，或者在沉浸式工坊中，听着白噪音专注工作。

本网站的所有故事、程序以及背景音乐，均由创作者独立编写与生成。我们的初衷，是为你打造一个随时可以回来的“深夜杂货铺”与“私人避难所”。

Welcome to MENG TALES

Montainment (MENG TALES) is an independent website dedicated to blending "Learning" and "Entertainment". We believe that learning and relaxing are not mutually exclusive. Here, you can take language quizzes, play memory games, read ongoing novels, or focus on your work with ambient sounds in our Immersive Studio.

All stories, programs, and background music on this site are independently written and generated by the creator. Our goal is to build a "late-night curio shop" and a safe haven that you can always return to.
